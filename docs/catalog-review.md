# Catalog review — October 1, 2026

48 selectable US card products. This is a manual, bundled catalog; no runtime scraping or API.

## Scope

Include recurring statement credits, portal discounts, expiring merchant credits, annual hotel/companion certificates and spend-earned annual certificates. Exclude signup bonuses, percentage cashback, insurance, status, generic property amenities, multi-year Global Entry credits and points-only renewal bonuses. Zero-fee cards require at least one recurring credit. Existing no-fee Hilton wallets remain accessible, but the product is excluded from the new-card picker.

Credits requiring enrollment, renewal or spending are conditional. Mark complete only after using the benefit. Certificate values and restricted Bilt Cash are zero in automatic cash recovery. The existing estimator treats a completed monetary period as fully used at face value; it does not track partial usage.

## Reset and valuation decisions

- Cardmember years, billing-cycle years, issue-dated TravelBank and certificates use `manual`: the user supplies bank-reported dates. They are not assumed to expire December 31. Separate $50 bookings and separate Instacart discounts have distinct stable IDs.
- Monthly United rideshare amounts retain December supplements; Quest/Business are $8 plus $4 extra in December, Club variants $12 plus $6. Instacart Quest is $10 + $5; Club is $10 + $10.
- IHG Food & Beverage vouchers expire at the end of their issuing quarter according to the current Chase offer details, unlike other IHG reward voucher types. Personal IHG retains its TravelBank perk and actual January 15/July 15 expiry. Current Business has different benefits and no assumed TravelBank perk.
- Delta Gold rideshare starts after the first renewal. The note makes eligibility explicit; hide the perk until eligible. Conditional Dell, Adobe, Delta Flight Credit and spend-earned certificates do not imply eligibility without reaching the stated spend requirement.
- Bilt Cash has redemption and expiry restrictions; $200 is shown as a noncash value label and does not increase earned cash. Relationship-specific Schwab/Morgan Stanley bonuses are custom perks because eligibility and amount depend on the account.
- Standard recurring fees are defaults, not a determination of the actual fee already paid. Promotional waivers and legacy-account fees can be edited per card.
- Limited-time promotions carry known end dates. Historical Saks entries remain available for existing completion records.

## Candidates deferred

Citi Prestige and U.S. Bank Altitude Reserve are legacy products whose current credit/reset rules could not both be confirmed from accessible issuer terms during this review. Their old published benefits are not copied as current. They can be added as custom cards using account terms. Southwest cards whose old annual travel credits were removed, and points-only anniversary cards, are outside this fixed-credit scope. This catalog is broad but is not claimed to contain every US card or every targeted account offer.
